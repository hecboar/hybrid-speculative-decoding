# E3.1 — partial-drafter frontier: interpretation

Falcon-H1-0.5B, val-test n=100, greedy, k in {1,2,4}. c_eff = MEASURED draft/full
forward-time ratio (CUDA events, use_cache=False, RTX PRO 4500 — non-L4, but the
ratio is GPU-agnostic per the metric caveat).

## Key numbers
| variant | c_param | c_eff | max S_theory |
|---|---|---|---|
| component_only (SSM drafter, the paper's method) | 0.784 | **0.984** | 0.87 (k=1) |
| component_only_partial 0.75 / 0.50 / 0.25 | 0.59/0.39/0.20 | 0.74/0.49/0.25 | 0.87/0.85/0.82 (k=1) |

## Reading (feeds the Day-5 ending decision)
1. **The paper's SSM drafter does NOT reach S_theory>1 on this path.** c_eff for
   component_only is 0.984 — removing the attention branch saves only ~1.6% of
   runtime, because the **naive-mamba SSM branch dominates the forward cost**
   (mamba-ssm/causal-conv1d kernels have no sm_120 wheels). So even with high
   acceptance (A_2=0.645, tau=1.29) the internal drafter is not cheap enough:
   max S_theory = 0.87 at k=1. This is the quantitative core of the
   **infrastructure-bottleneck thesis** and directly answers R2.6 (parameter-count
   c_param=0.784 badly overstates the drafter's cheapness vs measured c_eff=0.984).
2. **Only the DIAGNOSTIC attention_only crosses 1.0**, and only because zeroing
   the (un-accelerated) SSM makes the forward 5x cheaper (c_eff=0.19). This is an
   artifact of the missing mamba kernels and INVERTS the paper's premise (it
   drafts with attention, discarding the SSM). It is reported as a diagnostic,
   NOT as the credible speedup path.
3. **Implication:** the path to S_theory>1 for the SSM drafter runs through
   cheaper SSM execution (optimized kernels) — which lowers c_eff for
   component_only — NOT through the drafting principle, which already delivers the
   acceptance. Under HARD CONSTRAINT 1 (no kernel work) this is out of scope here,
   so on the L4/current-stack the honest result is **Ending B** unless E4 shows
   c_eff dropping with context length. Partial drafters trade acceptance away
   faster than cost, so none of them crosses 1.0 either.
